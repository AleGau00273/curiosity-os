03.3 / PROJECT SPOTLIGHT

Game Boy Advance Restoration

A personal restoration project that challenged me to go beyond basic repairs. I rebuilt a well-used original Game Boy Advance, learned more advanced soldering techniques, and gave the handheld a new lease on life.

GBA / RESTORATION ARCHIVE01 / 06

Before and After

I rebuilt a worn Game Boy Advance with a new shell and backlit screen, giving the original hardware a second life.

THE REBUILD

What I changed

I replaced the original non-backlit screen with a backlit display, fitted a replacement shell, soldered in a CleanAMP Pro audio amplifier and dehum/dehiss kit, and desoldered and replaced faulty L and R shoulder buttons. I also wired the screen ribbon to the Select, L, and R test points to control display settings such as brightness.

It was a bigger undertaking than I expected, but the experience helped me build confidence in soldering, careful disassembly, and troubleshooting. Seeing the finished console power on made the work worthwhile.
